Measures how you write in LibreOffice Writer (typing rhythm, pauses, corrections, pastes) and sends the counters to your Certimens space. No text and no keystroke is ever recorded: counters only. A Certimens account, provided by your institution, is required. From Writer's Certimens menu, the student links their document to a Certimens file, submits it to an assignment of their class, and sends its .docx version in one click.
